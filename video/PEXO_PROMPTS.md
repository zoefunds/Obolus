# PEXO_PROMPTS.md — Obolus

## Decision
The master film uses **no Pexo-generated cinematic footage**. Rationale: Obolus's
strongest material is its own real UI (4 screenshots) and its own real contract logic
(the fetch → validator reasoning → consensus mechanism). Per the brief's own guidance —
"no Pexo clip should appear merely because it looks cinematic" and "every generated clip
must support a specific narration line" — there was no scene in this storyboard where a
generic cinematic shot would out-perform a diagram built from the actual contract, or
where it wouldn't risk implying a fabricated product interaction (e.g. a "family
grieving" shot next to a death-verification product risks tastelessness, not credibility).

## Optional exception considered and rejected
A cold-open shot of "a phone left unanswered on a kitchen counter, days of dust
accumulating" was considered for Scene 1 as an alternative to the Remotion countdown
graphic. Rejected because: (a) it risks reading as generic stock/ad footage exactly as
the brief warns against, (b) the Remotion countdown ties directly and legibly into
Scene 2's "INACTIVITY TIMER" label via shared motion continuity, which a live-action shot
cannot do as cleanly, (c) it stays fully truthful with zero risk of misrepresenting
functionality.

## If a future cut wants one Pexo shot (not built into this version)
If revisited, the single defensible candidate is a **Scene 1 replacement**, prompt below
— kept here for reference only, not used in the current build:

- **Shot purpose:** cold-open contradiction, no product visible yet
- **Subject:** a smartphone lock screen, untouched
- **Environment:** a plain kitchen counter, soft daylight through a window, otherwise empty
- **Exact action:** static shot; only a subtle dust-mote drift and a slow, almost
  imperceptible light change to imply passing days
- **Camera framing:** tight overhead, phone centered, shallow depth of field
- **Camera movement:** none — locked-off shot, or an extremely slow 2% push-in only
- **Lighting:** warm natural daylight, gradually dimming to suggest time passing
- **Mood:** quiet, unresolved, not sombre or morbid
- **Colour relationship to product branding:** warm neutral tones only; no gold/cyan
  color grading applied here — those are reserved for product footage
- **Shot duration:** 4 seconds
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (crop variants for 9:16/1:1 if used)
- **Realism level:** photorealistic
- **Prohibited elements:** no visible notifications, no readable text/UI on the phone
  screen, no people, no crypto/blockchain imagery, no death-adjacent objects (no
  flowers, candles, obituary pages)
- **Transition compatibility:** must cut cleanly to a hard flash-cut, matching the
  current Remotion countdown's exit
